# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

## About the Movie
Spider-Man is one of my favorite superhero movies. 
It tells the story of Peter Parker, a young man who 
gets special powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

## Main Character
- Peter Parker (Spider-Man)
- Miles Morales
- Mary Jane Watson
- Green Goblin

## Why I Like This Movie
I like Spider-Man because he is brave, helpful, and 
always tries to protect other people. He teaches us 
that with great power comes great responsibility.

## My Favorite Part
My favorite part is when Spider-Man saves people 
and fights villains to protect the city.

## Conclusion
Spider-Man is an amazing movie because it teaches 
us about courage, responsibility, and helping others.
